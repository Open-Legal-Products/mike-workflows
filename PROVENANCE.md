# Provenance and Licensing

Every workflow must have a clear origin and a license that permits its use and
redistribution.

## Original Work

Original Open Legal Products workflows may use `license: "MIT"` and
`author: "Open Legal Products"`.

## Adapted or Third-Party Work

For copied or adapted material:

- Confirm that the upstream license permits redistribution and modification.
- Preserve all required copyright, attribution, patent, trademark, and notice
  text.
- Include a copy of every applicable third-party license in the repository.
- Preserve or add modification notices where required by the upstream license.
- Record the source URL, repository, revision, upstream path, author, and
  license in the workflow metadata or pack manifest.
- Do not label third-party material as MIT-only unless the copyright holder has
  granted that right.

For Apache-2.0 material, retain the Apache license, relevant copyright notices,
any required `NOTICE` content, and a clear statement that the files were
modified. The repository-level MIT license applies only where no more specific
license or notice applies.

Do not contribute material with unclear ownership, missing license terms, or
provenance that cannot be verified.
